How it works

Explore 4 easy notes — a comfortable low, a lower note, a comfortable high, and a higher note. Hold each one gently for about a second. You can repeat a comfortable note instead of stretching your range.

This is an optional range snapshot. Stay within easy notes, rest if your voice feels tired, and stop if singing feels strained or painful.

Best in a quiet room. Your voice never leaves this device — nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Quick question first…

Which notes usually feel easier? Choose a lower or higher range comparison, or compare with all ranges. This is a rough comparison, not an assignment of your voice type.
